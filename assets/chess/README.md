The six `.3mf` files and `Pawn.stl` are the original downloads, retrieved 2026-10-29. `source.json` records the author's published model and license metadata. These assets retain their own license and are **not** relicensed under the project's AGPLv3.

`unity/Assets/Editor/ChessImport.cs` converts their mesh data into Unity's format without removing or reshaping triangles. Coordinate axes, display units and origin are converted; the king's two supplied printable parts are assembled at its cross slot. White/black game pieces use display materials. Original downloads are provided here so the original geometry remains available independently of the app.

The work is provided as-is, without warranties; see the license's disclaimer. Attribution does not imply the author's endorsement of Movie Night. Do not distribute artistic remixes under this license without separate permission.

The author’s `Pawn.3mf` contains the same mesh as `Rook.3mf`. It is retained untouched for provenance; the importer uses the separately supplied **Pawn.stl** (54,134 original triangles) for the pawn. Exact duplicate STL vertices are indexed together without changing triangle positions or topology.
